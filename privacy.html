Privacy Policy

Lucky Learning Co. · Effective November 15, 2026

Lucky Learning Co. creates printable teaching resources sold on Teachers Pay Teachers (TPT). This policy explains what information we handle, including through our Pinterest tool, the Lucky Learning Co. Pin Planner.

Lucky Learning Co. Pin Planner

The Pin Planner is an internal tool that we use only for our own Pinterest business account. It creates Pins that show our own teaching resources and link to our product pages on TPT. Before any Pin is scheduled, a person at Lucky Learning Co. reviews and approves it.

  • It connects only to our own Pinterest account, with our permission (OAuth). It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account.
  • It does not collect, store or analyze personal information about Pinterest users.
  • The only Pinterest data it keeps is our own access token, stored securely on our computer, and the IDs of Pins we have published, so that we don't post duplicates.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer any Pinterest data to third parties, and we don't use it for advertising outside Pinterest or to train AI models.
  • If we stop using the tool, or Pinterest asks us to, we delete the data described above within 30 days.

Purchases and our TPT store

All purchases, downloads and accounts are handled by Teachers Pay Teachers. We never receive your payment details. Information TPT collects is covered by the TPT Privacy Policy. We receive the standard sales reports TPT provides to sellers. We use them only to run our store and never sell or share them.

Cookies and tracking

This page does not use cookies, analytics or tracking.

Children

Our resources are made for teachers to use with students, but this tool and this website are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
